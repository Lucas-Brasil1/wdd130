Class III Rapids

Feel the thrill of fast water on this 5-hour adventure. You will paddle through big waves and exciting rapids with an expert guide. Best for ages 12 and up. Safety gear, paddles, and a riverside snack are included.

River Trip

Enjoy a 4-hour ride through classic river rapids with a friendly, expert guide. You will paddle with your group, splash through waves, and stop for photos along the way. All safety gear and paddles are included.

Scenic Float

Relax on a calm 3-hour float down the river. This trip is perfect for families and first-time rafters. You will enjoy beautiful views, gentle rapids, and wildlife along the shore. Guide, helmet, and life jacket are included.
